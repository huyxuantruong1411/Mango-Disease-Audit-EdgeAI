## Abstract

Controlled studio plant pathology datasets frequently report near-ceiling classification accuracy (> 99%) using modern deep convolutional neural networks and vision transformers. However, transferring these monolithic models onto real-world agricultural edge platforms exposes three critical system vulnerabilities:
1. **Evaluation inflation via specimen leakage**, where sibling views of the same physical leaf populate both training and evaluation subsets, and studio backdrops introduce non-biological classification shortcuts.
2. **Optical perturbation fragility**, where unconditional enhancement degrades clean frames and amplifies high-frequency sensor noise.
3. **Edge compute bottlenecks and cross-domain shift**, where deep backbones require tens of megabytes of memory and 48 ms to 272 ms per frame on single-core edge CPUs yet collapse when evaluated on external field datasets.

In this paper, we present an **Audit-First, Quality-Gated Two-Stage Framework** (`mango_edge_cv`) evaluated across **7,000 mango leaf images** (`MangoLeafBD`, $N = 4,000$, 8 classes; `MLDID`, $N = 3,000$, 5 shared classes):

- **Pillar I (Graph-Based Specimen Audit & Shortcut Quantification):** Dual-criterion connected-component clustering (64-bit DCT perceptual hash Hamming distance $\le 6$ or central-60% HSV histogram cosine similarity $\ge 0.992$) proves that **36.15% (1,446 / 4,000) of `MangoLeafBD` images are near-duplicate or zoom-rotated siblings across 2,554 unique specimen clusters**, and that naive random splitting leaks **39.0% of multi-image clusters** into the test set. A complementary 5-view shortcut probe reveals that **isolated studio background achieves 94.84% accuracy**, four $32 \times 32$ corner patches achieve **85.08%**, and 7D file metadata achieves **59.14%** (vs. 12.50% random chance).
- **Pillar II (Foreground-Aware Quality-Gated Optical Preprocessing):** A hybrid HSV saturation and CIE-LAB chromaticity salience segmenter isolates the biological leaf lamina prior to feature extraction, conditionally triggering Donoho–Johnstone Median Absolute Deviation ($\hat{\sigma}_{\text{MAD}}$) sensor-grain denoising, central-window specular flash glare replacement, and foreground-restricted $L^{\ast}$ CLAHE. Under $\sigma = 10$ Gaussian sensor noise, this quality gate delivers a **+0.3533 Macro-F1 gain** over raw features (0.8647 vs. 0.5114), whereas unconditional CLAHE collapses to 0.4207.
- **Pillar III (Shared-Sobel Seven-Branch Feature Engine & Block-PCA):** Reusing a single $3 \times 3$ Sobel gradient pass across 2,003 raw feature dimensions, our `BlockPCACompressor` resolves the **7.38:1 HOG dimensionality dilution** by compressing HOG to 64 principal axes and equalizing RMS energy ($\gamma = 0.45 \to 303\text{D}$), yielding a statistically significant **+5.86% accuracy jump** (92.05% in $M_9$ to **97.91%** in $M_{10}$, exact McNemar $p < 0.0001$).
- **Pillar IV (Temperature-Calibrated Two-Stage Confidence Gate):** A temperature-scaled Stage-1 linear specialist ($T^{\ast} = 0.758$, $\text{ECE} = 0.0094$) classifies **98.47% of test specimens on the fast path in 0.48 ms** of classifier time. Only **1.53% of ambiguous boundary cases** ($\tau_{\text{gate}} = 0.65$) are selectively escalated to a heterogeneous Stage-2 nonlinear ensemble (Platt-calibrated RBF-SVM, single-hidden-layer MLP, and cosine kNN), elevating accuracy to **98.47% (0.9836 Macro-F1)** with an ultra-compact **480.2 KB footprint** and selective risk $\text{AURC} = 0.00158$.
- **Pillar V (Edge CPU Profiling & Robustness):** End-to-end single-core edge CPU latency is **21.58 ms (46.3 FPS)** for Stage-1 and **21.74 ms (46.0 FPS)** for the gated system ($2.26\times\text{--}12.61\times$ faster than MobileNetV3-Small, MobileNetV2, and ResNet50).
- **Pillar VI (Cross-Dataset Generalization):** **Eigenvector Canonical Leaf Pose Normalization** improves zero-shot cross-dataset transfer on 3,000 external field images (`MLDID`) from **31.27% (0.2886 Macro-F1) to 42.63% (0.4395 Macro-F1)** (+11.36% accuracy and +15.09% Macro-F1 gain), decisively outperforming unsupervised CORAL (31.43%).
