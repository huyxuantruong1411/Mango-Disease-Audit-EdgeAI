## Abstract

Studio-captured plant pathology datasets frequently report near-ceiling classification accuracy ($99\%+$) using deep neural networks, yet suffer from severe accuracy degradation under field perturbations, cross-dataset domain shifts, and strict single-core edge latency budgets. In this paper, we present an **Audit-First, Quality-Gated Two-Stage Framework** for real-time mango leaf disease diagnosis across **$7,000$ images** (`MangoLeafBD`, $N=4,000$, 8 classes; `MLDID`, $N=3,000$, 5 shared classes). First, a dual-criterion connected-component graph audit ($64$-bit DCT perceptual hash $d_{\text{Hamming}} \le 6$ or central-$60\%$ HSV cosine similarity $\ge 0.992$) reveals that **$36.15\%$ ($1,446 / 4,000$)** of `MangoLeafBD` images are near-duplicate or zoom-rotated siblings across **$2,554$ unique specimen clusters**, and that naive random splitting leaks **$39.0\%$ of multi-image clusters** into the test set. A complementary 5-view background shortcut audit demonstrates that **masked studio background alone achieves $94.84\%$ group-split accuracy**, four $32\times32$ corner patches achieve **$85.08\%$**, and $7\text{D}$ file metadata achieves **$59.14\%$** (vs. $12.50\%$ random chance). Second, we introduce a foreground-aware quality-gated optical preprocessor combining hybrid Otsu–LAB chroma segmentation, a Donoho–Johnstone Median Absolute Deviation ($\hat{\sigma}_{\text{MAD}}$) sensor-grain gate, central-window specular glare replacement, and foreground-restricted $L^*$ CLAHE, yielding a **$+0.3533$ Macro-F1 gain** under $\sigma=10$ sensor noise where unconditional CLAHE collapses ($0.8647$ vs. $0.4207$). Third, we extract $2,003$ raw features across 7 biological branches reusing a single $3\times3$ Sobel pass and resolve the **7.38:1 HOG dimensionality dilution** ($86.61\%$ in $M_4$) via an **Energy-Balanced Block-PCA Compressor** ($303\text{D}$), driving a statistically significant **$+5.86\%$ accuracy jump** ($92.05\%$ in $M_9$ to **$97.91\%$** in $M_{10}$, exact McNemar $p < 0.0001$). Fourth, a temperature-calibrated ($T^* = 0.758$, $\text{ECE} = 0.0094$) Stage-1 linear specialist resolves **$98.47\%$ of test samples in $21.58\text{ ms}$ ($480.2\text{ KB}$)** on a single CPU thread, routing only **$1.53\%$** of ambiguous samples ($\tau_{\text{gate}} = 0.65$) to a heterogeneous Stage-2 nonlinear ensemble ($0.55\text{ RBF-SVM} + 0.30\text{ MLP} + 0.15\text{ Cosine-kNN}$) to reach **$98.47\%$ accuracy ($0.9836\text{ Macro-F1}$)** at **$21.74\text{ ms}$** mean latency ($2.26\times\text{--}12.61\times$ faster than MobileNetV3-Small, MobileNetV2, and ResNet50) and **$98.74\%$ accuracy** at $\tau_{\text{gate}} = 0.85$. Finally, **Eigenvector Canonical Leaf Pose Normalization** improves zero-shot cross-dataset accuracy on `MLDID` ($N=3,000$) from **$31.27\%$ ($0.2886\text{ Macro-F1}$) to $42.63\%$ ($0.4395\text{ Macro-F1}$)** ($+11.36\%$ accuracy and $+15.09\%$ Macro-F1 gain).
