Pillar I-A • Specimen-Identity Graph Audit (Table 1)
Graph-Based Near-Duplicate Discovery & Split-Leakage Proof on MangoLeafBD
Why naive random train/test splitting leaks 39.0% of multi-image specimen clusters, and how dual-criterion connected components enforce 0.0% leakage.
Criterion 1: 64-Bit Structural pHash 2D-DCT
dHamming(pHash(Ii), pHash(Ij)) ≤ 6 bits
1. Resize grayscale image to 32×32.
2. Compute 2D Discrete Cosine Transform DCT(I32).
3. Extract top-left 8×8 low-frequency block and threshold against the median of the 63 AC coefficients.
4. Captures identical leaves under re-compression, minor cropping, or brightness shifts.
Criterion 2: Central-ROI Color Cosine 96D HSV
cos(hHS(Ii), hHS(Ij)) ≥ 0.992
1. Crop central 60% ROI ([0.2H:0.8H, 0.2W:0.8W]) and resize to 64×64.
2. Compute joint 2D Hue–Saturation histogram (12 × 8 = 96 bins, L2-normalized).
3. Rotation-invariant descriptor that links 90°/180°/270° rotated and zoomed views of the same leaf.

Pillar II-B • Selective Optical Restoration (apply_adaptive_quality_preprocessing)
Foreground-Aware Quality-Gated Preprocessing: Visual & Quantitative Proof
Why unconditional enhancement degrades clean or noisy leaves, and how three mathematical gates selectively trigger restoration only when physical degradation is detected.
Gate 1: Donoho–Johnstone MAD Sensor Grain Gate (σ̂MAD = median(|R − median(R)|) / 0.6745) +0.3533 Macro-F1 at σ=10
1A. Sensor Grain (σ = 10): Raw baseline drops to 0.5114 F1; unconditional CLAHE amplifies grain and collapses to 0.4207 F1.
1B. 3×3 Median Residual |R|: Evaluated strictly on foreground Mfg. Triggers 2-tier smoothing at σ̂MAD ≥ 5.5 and ≥ 10.0.
1C. MAD-Gated Output: Preserves clean leaves untouched (σ=0: 0.9792 F1) while restoring σ=10 accuracy to 0.8647 F1.
Gate 2: Central-Window Specular Flash Glare Attenuation (V > 238 ∧ S < 38 in Central 72% ROI) 0.9770 Macro-F1 at r=35px
2A. Specular Flash Glare: High-intensity achromatic spots create false step-edges that corrupt Sobel/HOG gradients.
2B. Central 72% Gate: Ignores outer white backdrop; triggers when foreground glare fraction ∈ [0.3%, 22.0%].
2C. Local Gaussian Fill: Dilates glare mask (5×5) and replaces with 11×11 neighborhood (σ=2.5), achieving 0.9770 F1.
Gate 3: Foreground-Restricted CIE L* Percentile Stretch & Mild CLAHE (σ(L*) < 38 or P95−P5 < 132) +0.1194 Macro-F1 at -40% Light
3A. Under-Exposed Leaf (−40% Light): Compresses dynamic range, causing raw baseline Macro-F1 to drop to 0.8391.
3B. Foreground [P2, P98] → [12, 242] + CLAHE (clip=1.55): Restores Macro-F1 to 0.9585 without touching background.

Pillar III-B • Variance-Balanced Subspace Compression (BlockPCACompressor)
Resolving High-Dimensional HOG Variance Dilution via Energy-Balanced Block-PCA
Mathematical and empirical explanation of why naive feature concatenation fails (M4: 86.61%) and how Block-PCA achieves 97.91% accuracy (M10, p < 0.0001).
The Problem: Dimensionality Dilution M4 Drops to 86.61%
1,764D HOG (88.1%) vs. 239D Color/Tex (11.9%)
Imbalance Ratio = 7.38 : 1
• Without HOG, compact Color + LBP (M2, 80D) achieves 97.35% Acc.
• Appending raw 1,764D HOG (M4, 1,844D) causes accuracy to collapse by −10.74% down to 86.61%!
• Even with all 7 branches + Quality Gate (M9, 2,003D), raw concatenation reaches only 92.05% Acc because isotropic L2 penalty λ||w||22 is overwhelmed by 1,764 noisy spatial-cell gradients.
The Solution: Energy-Balanced Block-PCA M10 Jumps to 97.91%
z = [ x̃ct (239D)  ;  αhog · PCA64(x̃hog) (64D) ] ∈ ℜ303
αhog = 0.45 · RMS(x̃ct) / RMS(zhog)
• Step 1: Preserve all 239D Chromatic-Texture & Quality descriptors at 100% fidelity via Z-score standardization.
• Step 2: Compress 1,764D HOG onto its top k = 64 unwhitened principal axes (27.6× reduction).
• Step 3: Scale HOG-PCA by RMS energy factor αhog (γ = 0.45), yielding a +5.86% accuracy jump (92.05% → 97.91%, McNemar p < 0.0001).
