Why Un-Audited Random Splits Inflate Performance: Across MangoLeafBD's 4,000 images, our specimen-clustering audit discovers only 2,554 unique leaf clusters (36.15% near-duplicate / augmentation-mix ratio, reaching 93.8% in Cutting Weevil and 81.0% in Die Back). Under a standard random split, 39.0% of test images share a specimen cluster with training data. Enforcing StratifiedGroupKFold eliminates this leakage completely, while our Five-View Shortcut Audit (Table 2) proves segmented leaves alone retain 94.84% Acc (0.9440 F1) and M11 achieves 96.37% Acc (0.9549 F1) without background cues.
Pillar 2 • Quality Gate

Foreground-Aware Optical Restoration

MAD Noise Gate (σ̂MAD ≥ 5.5) + Glare Attenuation + Foreground CLAHE

Low-Light + Grain + Specular Glare
Quality-Gated Restored ROI
Preventing Perturbation Collapse Without Distorting Clean Leaves: Unconditional CLAHE severely amplifies sensor noise (σ=10 F1 drops from 0.5114 to 0.4207). By conditioning median/Gaussian denoising strictly on σ̂MAD ≥ 5.5 and local glare replacement on central foreground glare masks (V>238, S<38), our Quality-Gated pipeline preserves clean test accuracy (0.9836 F1) while delivering massive gains under field degradations (Table 5): +0.3533 F1 at Noise σ=10 (0.8647) +0.2359 F1 at Glare r=35 (0.9770) +0.1371 F1 at Low-Light -40%.
Pillar 3 • Domain Shift

Canonical Leaf Pose Normalization

PCA Major-Axis Horizontal Alignment + Foreground L* Constancy (lab_l)

MLDID Portrait Leaf (240×293)
Canonical Horizontal Pose (0°)
Diagnosing and Resolving Cross-Dataset Orientation Shift: EDA reveals that MangoLeafBD leaves are captured predominantly in landscape (320×240, aspect ratio 1.33), whereas external MLDID leaves (N=3,000 across 5 shared classes) are captured in portrait (240×293, aspect ratio 0.82), misaligning 8×8 HOG cells and 5-Patch quadrants. Rotating every segmented leaf organ to its principal inertia axis and normalizing foreground L* boosts zero-shot cross-dataset accuracy without any target labels (Table 7): Zero-Shot Acc: 31.27% → 42.63% (+11.36%) Macro-F1: 0.2886 → 0.4395 (+0.1509).
